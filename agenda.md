      <td>12:00 CET<br>1 hour</td>
      <td>Anonymizing research data with Amnesia</td>
      <td><a href="https://dtudk.zoom.us/j/61471652223">Zoom meeting</a></td>
      <td>
        <p>GDPR strictly limits how personal data can be used and shared, while properly anonymized data fall outside its scope and can be shared without consent. This webinar explains what anonymization is, why pseudonymization is not enough, and how guarantees such as k-anonymity and k^m-anonymity protect individuals against re-identification. We then demonstrate <a href="https://amnesia.openaire.eu/">Amnesia</a>, the free, open-source anonymization tool offered through OpenAIRE, on an example dataset, and discuss its limitations and how it compares with synthetic data.</p>
        <p><strong>Format:</strong> Online presentation with a live demo and Q&amp;A<br>
        <strong>Audience:</strong> Researchers, PhD students, data stewards and research data management staff who work with personal or sensitive data.<br>
        <strong>Prior knowledge:</strong> None required. Familiarity with tabular data is helpful; no programming or prior anonymization knowledge is needed.</p>
      </td>
      <td><strong>Speaker:</strong> Dr. Manolis Terrovitis, Research Director, IMSI, Athena Research Center, Greece<br>
        <strong>Organiser:</strong> Jitka Stilund Hansen, DTU</td>
